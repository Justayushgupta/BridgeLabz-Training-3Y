Typography and Text Styling

Introduction

Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed. Good typography sets the tone of the content and significantly affects user experience on the web.

Different font families convey different emotions. Bold text creates emphasis and draws attention. Italic text is traditionally used for titles, technical terms, or foreign words. The combination of both creates a visually rich reading experience.

Code Sample

font-family: Georgia, serif;
font-size: 1.1rem;
line-height: 1.9;
letter-spacing: 0.5px;

Key Principles

Line height, letter spacing, and text alignment work together to make text readable and beautiful. Contrast between headings and body text helps guide the reader's eye through the content naturally and effectively.
